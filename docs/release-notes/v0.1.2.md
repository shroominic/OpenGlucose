# OpenGlucose 0.1

OpenGlucose 0.1 is the first stable 0.1 release of the local-first CGM wellness
app for Android and iPhone.

## What's new

- More reliable Android sensor setup, including phones that require pairing or
  a fresh Bluetooth connection before service discovery.
- A clearer 60-minute sensor warmup experience. Early warmup values stay out
  of the dashboard, wellness summaries, Apple Health, and live glucose
  surfaces.
- Warmup countdowns in Android notifications and iOS Live Activities.
- Optional post-warmup glucose values in live notifications and Live
  Activities. Sensitive values remain hidden by default.
- Clearer Bluetooth-off, scan, pairing, range, and disconnect recovery without
  exposing native error details.
- Safer retry behavior that does not automatically unpair or reset an active
  sensor.

## Pairing note

An AiDEX transmitter can be paired to only one phone at a time. Keep other
phones and the official sensor app disconnected while moving an active sensor
to OpenGlucose. Do not unpair or reset a working sensor unless a reviewed
recovery procedure specifically asks for it.

OpenGlucose is wellness/reference software, not a medical device. Do not use it
for diagnosis, medication or insulin dosing, treatment decisions, or emergency
monitoring.

App version: `0.1.2` (build `22`)
