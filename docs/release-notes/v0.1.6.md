# OpenGlucose 0.1.6

OpenGlucose 0.1.6 adds Simplified Chinese and fixes archived sensor export on
iPhone.

## What's new

- Use OpenGlucose in English or Simplified Chinese.
- Follow the phone's preferred supported language by default, or select a
  language in Settings.
- Show localized OpenGlucose content in Android live notifications and iOS
  Live Activities.

## Fixed

- Archived sensor export now opens the Apple share sheet from the sensor
  archive detail screen.
- Export shows clear progress, prevents duplicate attempts, and safely removes
  temporary or partial files after completion or failure.
- A failed export shows an identifier-free support code that can be copied for
  troubleshooting.

## Download

On Android, expand **Assets** and download `openglucose-0.1.6+28.apk`.

OpenGlucose is wellness/reference software, not a medical device. Do not use it
for diagnosis, medication or insulin dosing, treatment decisions, or emergency
monitoring.

App version: `0.1.6` (build `28`)
