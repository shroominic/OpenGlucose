# OpenGlucose v0.1.0 Beta

OpenGlucose v0.1.0 is an early beta of the local-first CGM wellness and
reference app.

## Highlights

- A reorganized full-screen Settings area with sensor lifecycle information,
  integrations, display controls, and advanced/developer options in clearer
  locations.
- A sensor archive that preserves previous sessions and their readings after a
  sensor expires or is disconnected.
- Single-file archive export in CSV, readable TXT, or genuine XLSX format, with
  a preview and identifier-minimizing defaults.
- A compact active-sensor expiry indicator on Home, instead of a large
  lifecycle card taking over the dashboard.
- Sample data that is clearly marked as a demo and is offered only to people
  without retained glucose history.
- Window-aware patterns and richer weekly recaps that withhold conclusions when
  there are not enough readings.
- Safer sensor activation, reconnect, expiry, history deduplication, and
  persisted-state handling.
- Local-first persistence, write-only Apple Health glucose export, and optional
  BYO-key cloud AI settings that remain disabled by default.

## Known limitations

- Complete all-data/recovery export, delete-all, and configurable retention
  flows are not implemented. This beta exports one archived sensor session at
  a time.
- Apple Health sleep, exercise, and imported historical glucose overlays are
  planned but not included in this beta.
- Local on-device model downloads and llama.cpp inference are planned but not
  included in this beta.
- The web demo uses browser `localStorage` and is not a supported private
  health-data store.
- Automated physical-device end-to-end coverage is still deferred; native
  Android and iOS builds and tests are separate release evidence.

OpenGlucose is early-stage wellness/reference software, not a medical device.
Do not use it for diagnosis, medication or insulin dosing, treatment decisions,
or emergency monitoring.
