# OpenGlucose 0.1

OpenGlucose 0.1 is the stable release of the local-first CGM wellness app for
Android and iPhone.

## What's new

- More reliable Android sensor setup on phones where bonding, service
  discovery, or notification setup can fail in a device-specific order.
- OpenGlucose stops scanning before it connects, lets each Bluetooth operation
  finish, and makes at most one fresh-connection recovery attempt.
- Sensor setup follows the required notification and authentication order,
  keeps an existing healthy pairing, and cannot repeat sensor activation.
- A new **Move sensor to another phone** action safely releases the current
  sensor pairing after an explicit warning. Normal **Disconnect** continues to
  preserve the pairing.
- A clearer 60-minute sensor warmup experience. Early warmup values stay out
  of the dashboard, wellness summaries, Apple Health, and live glucose
  surfaces.
- Warmup countdowns in Android notifications and iOS Live Activities.
- Optional post-warmup glucose values in live notifications and Live
  Activities. Sensitive values remain hidden by default.
- Clearer Bluetooth-off, scan, pairing, range, timeout, and disconnect
  recovery without exposing native error details.

## Before connecting

An AiDEX transmitter can be paired to only one phone at a time. Keep other
phones and the official sensor app disconnected while OpenGlucose connects.
Before changing phones, use **Move sensor to another phone** on the phone that
is currently connected. Do not remove the Android pairing or reset an active
sensor unless specific recovery guidance asks for it.

## Download

On Android, expand **Assets** and download `openglucose-0.1.3+24.apk`.

OpenGlucose is wellness/reference software, not a medical device. Do not use it
for diagnosis, medication or insulin dosing, treatment decisions, or emergency
monitoring.

App version: `0.1.3` (build `24`)
